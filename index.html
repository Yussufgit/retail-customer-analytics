Where a UK online retailer's revenue actually comes from

RFM segmentation and cohort retention analysis on 531,282 real transactions (Dec 2010 – Dec 2011, 4,340 customers, 38 countries). Built with SQL (DuckDB), Python, and this dashboard.

of all revenue comes from just 31% of customers

This "Champions" segment — 1,358 customers who buy often and recently — is confirmed two independent ways: RFM segmentation and a simple revenue-quintile split (top 20% of customers drive 78.8% of revenue). A win-back campaign for the 138 "At Risk" customers, who used to buy this often but have gone quiet for 200+ days on average, is the clearest lever in this data.
